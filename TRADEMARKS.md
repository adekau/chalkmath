# The ChalkMath name and logo

ChalkMath's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): anyone may use, modify,
host and redistribute it, with credit (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The license does not cover the
**name "ChalkMath" or the logo** (`apps/notebook/assets/logo.svg`); this page does. It follows the
model of projects like Jupyter: host it, build on it, say what it is, don't pass off something else
as it or it as something else.

**Go ahead, no need to ask:**

- Host ChalkMath for your class, school, company or anyone else, unmodified or with changes that
  don't change what it is (configuration, deployment, bug fixes, translations), and call it
  ChalkMath.
- Say that your product or service is "based on ChalkMath", "powered by ChalkMath" or "runs
  ChalkMath notebooks", and link here.
- Use the name and logo to write or talk about ChalkMath, and in teaching materials that use it.

**Please don't:**

- Release a modified version under the name ChalkMath, or with the logo, when it has changed what
  ChalkMath is. Give it its own name, and say it is based on ChalkMath.
- Remove the credit to ChalkMath from a copy you host or distribute and present it as your own
  work.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the ChalkMath project endorses or runs your product
  when it doesn't, or in your own product's name or domain.

If you're unsure whether something is fine, open an issue at
https://github.com/adekau/chalkmath/issues and ask.
