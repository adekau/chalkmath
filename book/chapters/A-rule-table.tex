\rt{simp.flatten}{verified}{Associativity of $+$ and $\cdot$. The pipeline also opens a whole chain of sums in a sum, or of products in a product, in one silent step before working inside it (\lc{openChain_soundR}).}

\rt{simp.fold-constants}{verified}{Exact rational arithmetic; $\mathbb{Q}$ embeds in $\mathbb{R}$.}

\rt{simp.collect-powers}{verified}{$b^m b^n \cdots = b^{m+n+\cdots}$, every factor with the same base in one step, for every real $b$: each merge has integer exponents of one sign ($x \cdot x = x^2$) or a positive numeral base (\lc{collectPowers_soundR}).}
\rt{simp.collect-powers.assuming}{conditional}{The other merges, $x \cdot x^{-1} = x^0$ and $x^a x^b = x^{a+b}$: the step states its assumption, $b \neq 0$ where every merge has integer exponents and $b > 0$ otherwise (\lc{collectPowersAssuming_soundR_on}); at $x = 0$, $x \cdot x^{-1}$ turns $0$ into $1$ (\lc{not_collectPowersAssuming_soundR}).}

\rt{simp.function.real}{verified}{$\ln e^x = x$, and $\ln b^p = p \ln b$ for an odd integer $p$, for every real number, keeping the domain (\lc{functionReal_soundR}); off over $\mathbb{C}$, where $\ln e^{4i} = (4 - 2\pi)i$ (\lc{not_functionReal_soundC}).}
\rt{simp.function.assuming}{conditional}{$e^{\ln x} = x$, and $\ln b^p = p \ln b$ for $p$ not an odd integer: the step states $x > 0$ or $b > 0$ (\lc{functionAssuming_soundR_on}); $\ln x^2$ is defined at $x = -2$ and $2 \ln x$ is not.}
\rt{simp.radical}{verified}{A perfect-power base is reduced ($8^{1/2} = 2^{3/2}$), same-index radicals multiply under one root, and $\sqrt{18}$ is written $18^{1/2}$, shown as $3\sqrt 2$; the searches are bounded by bit length ($\sqrt{10^{401}} = 10^{401/2}$), leaving a number of 4096 bits or more as it is; unconditional, the bases being positive integers.}
\rt{simp.sqrt}{verified}{$\sqrt a = a^{1/2}$ for every real $a$. Silent: the two print alike.}
\rt{simp.collect-radicals}{verified}{Radicals with the same square-free part collect, $\sqrt{50} - \sqrt{18} = 2\sqrt 2$; the square-free part comes from trial division, exact below $2^{60}$ for square roots; unconditional.}
\rt{simp.sort}{verified}{Commutativity: the arguments of $+$ and $\cdot$ are put in a canonical order (\lc{SemEqR.canon}). Silent: it reorders only.}

\rt{cmd.expand}{verified}{Distribution is a total function proved sound over $\mathbb{R}$ (\lc{dist_sound}); the collection afterwards is the pipeline's own steps with their statuses.}
\rt{cmd.simplify}{verified}{A cell's argument is already in normal form when the command sees it, so \texttt{simplify} returns it unchanged: the identity.}
\rt{cmd.subst}{verified}{Structural substitution $e[x := v]$ evaluates as $e$ with $x$ rebound to the value of $v$ (\lc{cmdSubst_soundR}).}
\rt{cmd.N}{verified}{The decimal is certified: interval arithmetic over the rationals holds the exact value (\lc{ieval_sound}), with Taylor remainders for $\exp$, $\sin$, $\cos$ on sums taken in interval arithmetic (\lc{expSumI_mem}, \lc{trigSumI_mem}), $\ln$ checked by $\exp$ and $\sqrt{\ }$ (and $x^{1/2}$) by squaring, and the digits shown are within a unit of their last place (\lc{certify_sound}, \lc{cmdN_sound}). $\arctan$ is pinned by $\tan$ (\lc{atanCheck_mem}). A complex value is certified part by part, a rectangle holding the principal value (\lc{cieval_sound}, \lc{cmdN_soundC}), $\ln z$ and $b^e = e^{e \ln b}$ through an argument certified by $\arctan$ (\lc{clnI_mem}).}

Expanding the answer gives the input's value back under the same conditions, not its term (\lc{expand_factor_sound}). When the check fails, the collected normal form is the answer.}
\rt{expand.distribute}{verified}{Multiplying out a product of sums and collecting like monomials: \lc{dist_sound}.}
\rt{expand.power}{verified}{A power of a sum is the sum multiplied by itself: \lc{dist_sound}.}
\midrule \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Integration}} \\
\rt{cmd.integrate}{verified}{Accepted only when the candidate's derivative normalizes to the integrand, exactly (\lc{cmdIntegrate_spec}); \lc{integrate_deriv} reads that as $F' = f$ wherever the differentiation steps shown are sound. \rt{int.check}{verified}{The differentiation of the candidate: this step carries the claim, with the statuses of its own steps.}
\rt{int.compare}{verified}{Derivative and integrand are rewritten with $\cos^2 u = 1 - \sin^2 u$, $(e^u)^k = e^{ku}$, $1/\sqrt{s} = s^{-1/2}$ and, under a negative power, $s^k = c^k (s/c)^k$ for a positive constant summand $c$ (\lc{identNorm}, \lc{ev_identPow}), expanded (\lc{dist}) and simplified before comparison; every rewrite is proved sound, and the statuses of the simplification steps apply.}
\rt{int.constant}{verified}{$\int c\,dx = c x$ for a term free of $x$, at every point (\lc{antiConst_sound}). Each finder rule is proved compositionally: if the sub-results are antiderivatives of the sub-integrands, so is the result, and \lc{anti_sound} puts the rules together, so an answer whose steps are all verified or conditional is an antiderivative wherever what they assume holds, before any check.}
\rt{int.variable}{verified}{$\int x\,dx = x^2/2$, at every point (\lc{antiVar_sound}).}
\rt{int.sum}{verified}{Given antiderivatives of the summands, their sum is one of the sum (\lc{antiSum_sound}); what the summands' own steps assume, they state.}
\rt{int.constant-multiple}{verified}{The factors free of $x$ times an antiderivative of the rest (\lc{antiConstMul_sound}).}
\rt{int.power}{verified}{$\int u^n\,du = u^{n+1}/(n+1)$ for a natural numeral $n$, at every point, through the chain rule in $u = a x + b$ (\lc{powerRule_sound}, \lc{linearCoeff_spec}).}
\rt{int.power.assuming}{conditional}{$u > 0$ for $\int u^{-1}\,du = \ln u$ and for a non-integer exponent, $u \neq 0$ for a negative integer one, $u > 0$ and $n \neq -1$ for a symbolic $n$, as the step states (\lc{powerRule_sound}); $\int x^{-1}\,dx = \ln x$ fails at $x = 0$, where $\ln$ has no derivative (\lc{not_power_sound}).}
\rt{int.exponential}{verified}{$\int b^u\,du = b^u/\ln b$ for a numeral base $b > 0$, $b \neq 1$, or $\pi$ (\lc{exponential_sound}).}
\rt{int.exponential.assuming}{conditional}{Any other base: the step states $b > 0$ and $b \neq 1$ (\lc{exponential_sound}); at $b = 1$ the candidate is $0$ and the integrand $1$ (\lc{not_exponential_sound}).}
\rt{int.table}{verified}{$\sin$, $\cos$, $\exp$ and $\arctan$ of $u = a x + b$, at every point (\lc{table_sound}, \lc{antiFn_sound}).}
\rt{int.table.assuming}{conditional}{$u > 0$ for $\ln u$, $\cos u > 0$ for $\tan u$, $-1 < u < 1$ for $\arcsin u$ and $\arccos u$, $\cos u \neq 0$ for $\sec^2 u$, as the step states (\lc{table_sound}, \lc{antiSecSq_sound}); $\int \sec^2 x\,dx = \tan x$ fails at $\pi/2$ (\lc{not_secSq_sound}).}
\rt{int.linear-substitution}{verified}{Dividing by a numeral coefficient $a \neq 0$ of $u = a x + b$: if $G(u)$ has derivative $g a$, then $G(u)/a$ has derivative $g$ (\lc{substitute_sound}).}
\rt{int.linear-substitution.assuming}{conditional}{A coefficient that is not a numeral: the step states $a \neq 0$ (\lc{substitute_sound}); at $k = 0$, $\sin(kx)/k$ is $0$ while $\cos(kx)$ is $1$ (\lc{not_substitute_sound}).}
\rt{int.substitution}{checked}{A guess from the finder ($u$-substitution), verified by \rn{int.check}. It is built from the normalizer's outputs ($g'$ and the ratio of the other factors to it), about which nothing is proved here, so the finder marks it and \lc{anti_sound} claims nothing for it.}
\rt{int.by-parts}{checked}{A guess from the finder (integration by parts), verified by \rn{int.check}. The $du$ and the $v\,du$ it integrates next are the normalizer's outputs, so the finder marks it and \lc{anti_sound} claims nothing for it.}
\rt{int.trig-power}{verified}{The reduction formulas for $\sin^m u \cos^n u$ with a numeral coefficient of $u$, given an antiderivative of the integrand left, by $\sin^2 u + \cos^2 u = 1$ (\lc{trigPower_sound}).}
\rt{int.trig-power.assuming}{conditional}{A coefficient $k$ that is not a numeral: the step states $k \neq 0$ (\lc{trigPower_sound}); at $k = 0$ the formula gives $x/2$ for $\int \sin^2(kx)\,dx$, the integral of $0$ (\lc{not_trigPower_sound}).}
\rt{int.exp-power}{verified}{$(e^u)^k = e^{ku}$ for every real $u$ and $k$, distributed by \lc{dist}: the integrand keeps its value (\lc{antiExpPower_sound}).}
\rt{int.arctan}{checked}{A guess from the finder ($1/(k + c u^2)$ as an arctangent), verified by \rn{int.check}.}
\rt{int.arcsin}{checked}{A guess from the finder ($1/\sqrt{k - c u^2}$ as an arcsine), verified by \rn{int.check}.}

\midrule \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Systems world}} \\
\rt{sys.init}{verified}{An initial state: the init condition evaluated on it, by definition.}
\rt{sys.step}{verified}{By definition: each step of a trace is re-run against the system, the action enabled there and its updates giving the next state; a step that does not re-run is refused, not shown.}
\rt{sys.found}{verified}{The last state of a re-run trace, from an initial state, where the formula holds. The trace is a shortest one: each state's breadth-first depth is zero initially and rises by at most one along a transition, checked, and no state where the formula holds is shallower than the trace is long, so every path to one is at least as long (\lc{checkShortest_spec}).}
\rt{sys.violated}{verified}{The last state of a re-run trace, from an initial state, where the formula fails: a concrete counterexample, and a shortest one, by the same check of breadth-first depths (\lc{checkShortest_spec}).}
\rt{sys.deadlock}{verified}{The last state of a re-run trace, with every action's guard evaluated false. When none is reported none is missed: the search finds every reachable state and every transition (\lc{System.no_deadlock}).}
\rt{sys.reach}{verified}{Breadth-first search from the initial states finds exactly the reachable states, each once (\lc{System.explore_states}).}
\rt{sys.invariant}{verified}{The formula holds in every state the search found, and the search finds every reachable state (\lc{System.explore_states}).}
\rt{sys.unreachable}{verified}{No state the search found satisfies the formula, and the search finds every reachable state (\lc{System.explore_states}).}
\rt{sys.inductive}{verified}{Every assignment of the domains is enumerated (\lc{allStates_mem}) and every enabled action from a state where the formula holds is checked.}
\rt{sys.cti}{verified}{By definition: a state where the formula holds and an action after which it fails (or leaves a domain), both evaluated by the system's own semantics, a concrete counterexample to induction.}
\rt{sys.ctl}{verified}{The set is a fixed point checked with a certificate read off its rounds, and where the check passes it is exactly the states where the formula holds by the meaning of its paths: $\mathrm{EF}$ some path reaches $\varphi$, $\mathrm{EG}$ an infinite path stays in it, $\mathrm{AG}$ every reachable state is in it, $\mathrm{AF}$ every maximal path reaches it (\lc{checkEF_spec} and the others).}
\rt{sys.iterate}{verified}{By definition: one round of the Kleene iteration, the transformer applied to the previous set; the rounds are the certificate \rn{sys.ctl} checks (\lc{checkEF_spec} and the others).}
\rt{sys.fixed}{verified}{The loop stops when a round changes nothing, and the set it stops at is checked against the operator's path meaning with \rn{sys.ctl}'s certificate, so it is the right set without appeal to Kleene's theorem.}
\rt{sys.cycle}{verified}{A step of the lasso's cycle, inside a strongly connected set of states avoiding the goal. The whole lasso is checked to be a run, each step an edge joined to the next and the cycle closing up (\lc{checkLasso_spec}).}
\rt{sys.lasso}{verified}{A fair run that never reaches the goal: a path from an initial state, then a cycle repeated forever, every weakly fair action taken on it or disabled somewhere on it and every strongly fair one taken on it or disabled all along it. A lasso that checks is a fair infinite run avoiding the goal (\lc{checkLasso_spec}); a deadlock reached first is checked as a run that stops without it (\lc{checkDead_spec}).}
\rt{sys.eventually}{verified}{Every fair run reaches the goal: checked with a certificate, a rank for each state reachable without the goal that no step raises, and where a run could stay level a helpful action, never taken there, that fairness forces; a strongly fair one hands the states where it is disabled to a further certificate. One that checks means every fair run reaches the goal (\lc{checkTrue_spec}, \lc{noFair}). The certificate comes from Emerson and Lei's search for Streett conditions.}
\rt{sys.refines}{verified}{Every initial state and every transition between reachable states is an edge the search found (\lc{System.explore_edges}), and each was mapped to an abstract initial state, step or stutter.}
\rt{trs.step}{verified}{An instance of one of the system's rules, rewritten at the position the step names (\lc{step\_sound}); the steps chain from the term to the answer (\lc{normalize\_chain}), and a normal form is one no rule applies to (\lc{normalize\_normal}).}
\rt{trs.decrease}{verified}{A rule passes when its left side's linear form has a larger constant and no smaller coefficient: then the left side is worth more whatever the variables are (\lc{decreases\_sound}), and every step lowers the term's value (\lc{step\_decreases}), so no term rewrites for ever (\lc{no\_infinite\_rewriting}). A rule that fails has values that make its left side worth no more (\lc{decreases\_complete}).}
\rt{trs.critical}{verified}{Robinson's unification, total by well-founded recursion, returns a most general unifier when there is one (\lc{unify_mgu}) and nothing only when there is none (\lc{unify_none}); \texttt{critical} pairs every rule with every rule at every non-variable position of the first one's left side, the variables renamed apart (\lc{apart_spec}), and records each overlap (\lc{mem_critical}), checked to be one (\lc{isPeak_sound}). Locally confluent when every pair joins: the critical pair lemma, the overlap below a variable joined by rewriting every copy of it (\lc{critical_pair_lemma}, \lc{critical_sound}), for every system \texttt{rules(…)} makes (\lc{parseSystem_wellFormed}). Not confluent when a pair's sides reach two different normal forms (\lc{critical_refutes}, \lc{step_complete}); confluent with termination by Newman's lemma (\lc{newman_lemma}). Sides that reach neither within the step limit leave it undecided.}
\rt{crdt.update}{verified}{A local update only raises entries of the replica's state (\lc{update\_inflation}).}
\rt{crdt.merge}{verified}{The entrywise maximum: a join, commutative, associative and idempotent (\lc{merge\_comm}, \lc{merge\_assoc}, \lc{merge\_idem}).}
\rt{crdt.send}{verified}{A message is a copy of the state; nothing to prove.}
\rt{crdt.converged}{verified}{Decided by comparing the states; equal states read equal values.}
\rt{crdt.diverged}{verified}{Decided by comparing the states.}
\midrule \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Logic world}} \\
\rt{logic.implication}{verified}{$a \to b$ and $\lnot a \lor b$ have the same value under every assignment, and the pass that applies it everywhere keeps the formula's value (\lc{arrows_sound}).}
\rt{logic.biconditional}{verified}{$a \leftrightarrow b$ and $(a \to b) \land (b \to a)$ have the same value under every assignment; the pass keeps the formula's value (\lc{arrows_sound}).}
\rt{logic.de-morgan}{verified}{$\lnot(a \land b) = \lnot a \lor \lnot b$ and its dual, over Bool; the negation pass keeps the value (\lc{nnf_sound}).}
\rt{logic.double-negation}{verified}{$\lnot\lnot a = a$ over Bool (\lc{nnf_sound}).}
\rt{logic.negate-constant}{verified}{$\lnot\top = \bot$ and $\lnot\bot = \top$ (\lc{nnf_sound}).}
\rt{logic.constants}{verified}{The identities and annihilators of $\land$ and $\lor$ over Bool (\lc{consts_sound}).}
\rt{logic.distribute}{verified}{$a \lor (b \land c) = (a \lor b) \land (a \lor c)$ and its dual over Bool (\lc{distrib_sound}, \lc{normal_sound}).}
\rt{logic.complement}{verified}{A term with $p$ and $\lnot p$ is false, a clause with them true, under every assignment (\lc{complement_sound}).}
\rt{logic.truthtable}{verified}{Every assignment to the formula's variables is a row (\lc{rows_complete}), and a formula's value depends only on its variables (\lc{eval_congr}): what holds in every row holds everywhere (\lc{taut_sound}, \lc{unsat_sound}), and a row returned as a witness is one (\lc{findRow_spec}).}
\rt{logic.evaluate}{verified}{A formula without variables: its value by definition of the connectives.}
\rt{logic.bounded}{verified}{A quantifier over a finite set is checked element by element, by definition; the atoms are evaluated by the pipeline, whose steps' statuses apply.}
\midrule \multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{$\lambda$-calculus}} \\
\rt{lambda.delta}{verified}{Unfolding a definition replaces a free name by its term; nothing to prove beyond that.}
\rt{lambda.beta}{verified}{Each step is one $\beta$-reduction of the de Bruijn terms: capture-avoiding substitution is de Bruijn substitution (\texttt{toDB\_subst}), for every strategy (\texttt{betaStep\_beta}, \texttt{cbnStep\_beta}, \texttt{cbvStep\_beta}, \texttt{appStep\_beta}, \texttt{betaEtaStep\_beta}).}
\rt{lambda.alpha-beta}{verified}{A binder renamed to avoid capture, then $\beta$: the renaming keeps the de Bruijn form (\texttt{toDB\_renameFor}) and leaves nothing to capture (\texttt{captureFree\_renameFor}), so the step is one $\beta$-reduction (\texttt{toDB\_subst}).}
\rt{lambda.elided}{verified}{The $\beta$-steps of a long reduction that the work does not show, each a step of the same strategy, so a $\beta$-reduction as \texttt{lambda.beta}'s are.}
\rt{lambda.eta}{verified}{$\eta$: $\lambda x.\, f\ x$ contracts to $f$ only when $x$ is not free in $f$ (\texttt{etaRedex\_spec}); $\eta$ is an axiom of $\lambda\beta\eta$, so nothing more to prove.}
\rt{lambda.fv}{verified}{The free variables, computed by their definition (\texttt{freeVars}).}
\rt{lambda.db}{verified}{De Bruijn indices, computed by their definition (\texttt{toDB}).}
\rt{lambda.alpha-eq}{verified}{$\alpha$-equivalence is taken to be equality of de Bruijn forms, which is how the engine defines it; that this agrees with renaming bound variables one at a time is the classical theorem, not proved here.}
\rt{lambda.alpha}{verified}{Binders renamed to avoid capture: the de Bruijn form is kept (\texttt{toDB\_renameFor}), so the term is the same up to $\alpha$.}
\rt{lambda.subst}{verified}{Substitution after the renaming captures nothing (\texttt{captureFree\_renameFor}) and is de Bruijn substitution (\texttt{toDB\_substRaw}).}
